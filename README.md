Cashflow – Your Money, Smarter
PROBLEM STATEMENT & PROPOSED SOLUTION

Problem Statement

Individuals face difficulties in managing their personal finances due to scattered financial records, poor expense tracking, missed bill payments, lack of budgeting discipline and limited understanding of their spending patterns.

Proposed Solution

Cashflow is an AI-powered personal finance management platform that integrates income and expense tracking, digital receipt storage, smart bill reminders, budget monitoring, savings goals and an intelligent AI assistant into a single user-friendly platform.

Core Innovation

Using AI-assisted financial analysis, Cashflow transforms financial records into meaningful insights and personalized suggestions, helping users make smarter financial decisions and improve their financial habits.
